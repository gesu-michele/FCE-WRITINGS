Recommended Title A Century of Progress: Is Modern Life Genuinely Superior?

Introduction It is frequently asserted that modern society enjoys far superior living standards compared to a century ago. While our ancestors undoubtedly led simpler and less frantic lives, substantial technological and social progress has unquestionably transformed human existence for the better.

Point 1: Health First and foremost, healthcare has experienced an astonishing revolution. One hundred years ago, curable bacterial infections frequently proved fatal, and average life expectancy was considerably lower. Today, thanks to cutting-edge medical breakthroughs, systematic vaccination programs, and sophisticated treatments, citizens live longer, healthier lives than ever before.

Point 2: Entertainment Point 3: Working Conditions (Own Idea) Furthermore, access to recreational activities and personal leisure has expanded drastically. Rather than enduring monotonous routines, people can effortlessly enjoy global cinema, literature, and digital media. More importantly, automated technology has liberated ordinary workers from exhausting manual labour, ensuring regulated working hours and far safer industrial environments.

Conclusion Taking all factors into account, I firmly maintain that life today is considerably more fulfilling. Although contemporary life entails certain environmental pressures, remarkable advances in medicine and working conditions render our era overwhelmingly preferable.

💡 Examiner Insight (Grade A / Band 5): 182 words total. Formal title, covers health, entertainment, and working conditions (original point 3), sophisticated discourse connectives, and strictly objective register without informal contractions.

🏛️ 6 Advanced Grammar Frames

1. Inversion (C1): "Hardly could our ancestors have imagined such breakthroughs."Difícilmente nuestros antepasados habrían imaginado tales avances.
2. Passive Reporting: "It cannot be denied that modern sanitation has saved millions."No se puede negar que el saneamiento moderno ha salvado a millones.
3. Cleft Sentence: "What has contributed most significantly to well-being is healthcare."Lo que más ha contribuido al bienestar humano es el sistema de salud.
4. Mixed Conditional: "Had these antibiotics not been discovered, mortality would still be rampant."Si no se hubieran descubierto estos antibióticos, la mortalidad aún sería alarmante.
5. Participle Clause: "Having mechanized heavy tasks, modern industry allows greater leisure."Habiendo mecanizado las tareas pesadas, la industria permite mayor tiempo libre.
6. Double Comparative: "The more medical science progresses, the longer citizens thrive."Cuanto más avanza la ciencia médica, más tiempo prosperan los ciudadanos.
